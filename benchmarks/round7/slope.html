Is more data still helping? Dev scores by training segment

Each segment is 500 training steps (4,000 chunks) on a growing mix, starting from the one before. Top row: the mean over the same 23 dev slices for every run, so the points are comparable. Bottom row: the W-2 and tax-form dev slices, which joined the mix at segment 3.

Reading it. Top row: from the round 6 model to segment 3, finds rose from 92.9% to 94.0% at the 0.40 threshold, but all of that came in segment 1 (94.5%); finds then drifted down about half a point while false hides kept falling, from 3.1% to 2.3%. So more of the same mix is trading finds for fewer false hides, not adding to both. Bottom row (0.40 threshold): segment 3 added generated W-2 and tax data, and the generated slices jumped to 98.5% and 99.5% while the hand-written ones stayed near 85%. The model learned my templates, not W-2s in general: variety, not volume, is what is missing. Segment 4 (real IRS-form and payroll-provider layouts, trained on W-2-focused data) dropped the top-row mean by about 1.5 points because the model forgot some general ability; it lifted the two W-2 layouts it was trained on (IRS form 94.3% to 98.9%, provider layouts 93.1% to 96.6% at the 0.35 threshold) but not the hand-written W-2s (88.1% to 86.4%), so the gap to real documents is still open.
